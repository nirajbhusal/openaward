About OpenAward

OpenAward is an independent, global index of Master's and PhD funding. We exist because the information that decides whether a talented student can afford to study is scattered across hundreds of university pages, embassy notices and LinkedIn posts, and most of it goes stale the week it is written.

Mission

Make every scholarship in the world findable, comparable and winnable by any qualified student, wherever they were born.

What we are

A verified listing index, a fit engine that scores your profile, an academy that closes the gaps, and a community of applicants who tell the truth about each award.

What we are not

Not an agent, not a consultancy, not an application portal. You always apply on the funder's own site. We never charge seekers to search.

How we work
Verify, then publishEvery listing is checked against its official page, stamped with a date, and re-checked every 30 days. If we cannot verify it, it does not go live.
One template for everythingLevel, fields, deadline, funding, English test, admission-first, who may apply. Same shape for a government award and a university fellowship, so you can compare.
Honest reviews onlyOnly people who marked "applied" or "awarded" can rate a scholarship. No funder can edit or remove a review.
Your data stays yoursThe fit score runs in your browser first. Sharing your profile with a funder is always opt-in, and you can export or delete everything.
Why it started

OpenAward began in Kathmandu in late 2026 as a side project by Niraj Bhusal, a civil servant working on digital public infrastructure, who studied abroad on a scholarship himself and watched friends miss deadlines they never knew existed.

The first version was a spreadsheet of nine bilateral scholarships. The second was a LinkedIn list with 269 likes and a comment nobody could answer: "which of these don't need IELTS?" OpenAward is the answer to that comment, built for every student, not just one country.

How we stay free for seekers

Search, fit score, alerts and fundamentals are free forever. Revenue comes from paid Academy intensives, funder plans (featured listings, analytics, sponsored courses) and qualified referrals to partner universities. Funders never buy a better rating or a higher position in search.
